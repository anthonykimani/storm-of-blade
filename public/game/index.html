Clash of Steel: Arena Duel

First-person sword duels with physics-based clashes - 900 bosses across 5 arenas (Three.js + cannon-es)
  • Levels: earn XP from victories; level up for more HP, stamina and damage — bosses scale to your level.
  • Bad ending: lose all lives to see the Arena Claims Another screen — Rise Again or return to the menu.
  • Ending: defeat boss 900 for the Crown of Vaelen ending, stats, and the Crownblade reward.
  • Final boss: boss 900 is Vaelen, a 2.6× giant with a crown, ground-slam shockwave and 2600 HP.
  • Minibosses: every 10th boss is a gold-marked miniboss with +60% HP, +15% damage, and bonus gold/XP.
  • Bosses: six attack patterns — overhead cut, sweeping slash, lunging thrust, shield bash (guard break), spinning slash and double cut.
  • Blade Shop: press H or use the Blade Shop button — earn gold from bosses and golden orbs, then buy faster or harder-hitting swords.
  • Desktop: Click Enter Arena, then move the mouse to look (no dragging needed), click to slash, hold right-click to guard, Space dodge, F lock-on (boss or nearest training dummy), V or the 3rd Person button to switch view, R restart, N next boss. Arrow keys also look; -/= change sensitivity.
  • Lives: you start with 3 hearts. Falling costs one life and restarts the duel; win to recover a heart (max 3). R restarts the current boss and refills lives.
  • Bonus stage: after winning a duel, press B to enter the Treasure Vault — slash 8 golden orbs in 20 seconds for +1 life (or a full heal if lives are full).
  • Training Grounds: use the menu button or press T to practise against straw dummies — no lives at risk. Every 4 dummy hits permanently sharpens your sword (+2 damage, max +30). Press T, Esc, or Leave Training to return.
  • Performance: if the game feels slow, press G to toggle Performance mode (shadows off, lower resolution). It also enables automatically on slow devices.
  • Mobile: joystick to move, drag right side to look, buttons to fight (Guard raises shield).
  • Pointer lock (mouse captured) is used automatically when available; otherwise free mouse-look is used.
Tip: for best results run via a local web server. Mobile works with on-screen controls.
